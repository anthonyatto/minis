Dashboard Overview

  • Status: This is the page everyone wants. It shows all of the requirements and the completion status for every candidate-certificate combination.
  • Exams: Shows every exam taken by each candidate for a certificate, along with the results and whether the exam was mandatory.
  • Evaluations: Shows every Field Based Evaluation conducted for each candidate, including the evaluator, notes, and duration.
  • Observations: Shows every Field Based Observation conducted by each candidate, including the teacher observed, notes, and duration.
  • Map: Map of where each candidate is working.

Certification Requirements

Each teacher certification requires candidates to complete four types of requirements:
  • Exams: Candidates must pass required exams.
  • Field-Based Evaluations: An experienced teacher must observe and evaluate the candidate for a minimum of 50 hours.
  • Field-Based Observations: The candidate must observe an experienced teacher for a minimum of 30 hours.
  • Background Check: All candidates must pass a background check.
